This track is an incredibly moody, emotionally raw piece of electronic dark-pop/melodic-bass. The lyrics paint a powerful narrative of emotional gravity, dependency, and the surreal feeling of drowning in love ("You hold me down, I'd rather love than be alone... cause I'll keep diving till I'm gone").

As Creative_Director_AI (Planning Mode), I have designed a complete, beat-synced visual storyboard for this song.

We will use a visual style called "Surrealist Neon-Noir & Oceanic Melancholy." Think of a blend between the high-contrast neon aesthetics of Euphoria, the atmospheric scale of Blade Runner 2049, and gravity-defying underwater sequences.

**Visual Direction & Core Motifs**

Color Palette:
Midnight Indigo (#0B1021) — representing isolation and the abyss.
Electric Violet (#8A2BE2) — representing the electric, intoxicating pull of the relationship.
Crimson Red (#DC143C) — representing the "burn," the blindfold, and the phantom heartbeat.
Amber Flare (#FFBF00) — representing warm, fleeting memories.
Key Themes: Gravity-defying water, floating shattered glass, glowing biological elements (the hollow chest cavity), and shadowy hands representing emotional restraint.

**Beat-Synced Visual Storyboard**

0:00 – 0:13 | Scene 1: The Descent (Intro)
Lyrics: "Our first days are gone / Now I'm making it still / It burns... when I think of you"
Visual: A figure (the protagonist) is suspended horizontally in a vast, dark, liquid void. Strands of dark hair and clothing drift weightlessly. As the line "It burns" plays, a soft, warm crimson glow ignites deep within their chest, pulsing under their skin like a dying ember.
Camera Movement: Overhead crane shot, slowly lowering and rotating clockwise over the protagonist.
Lighting: Deep indigo background with a single overhead spot of cold violet light. A sudden amber-crimson light leaks from beneath the protagonist's collarbone.

0:13 – 0:27 | Scene 2: The Fracture (Verse 1)
Lyrics: "Love shot me blind, ready to kill / You ripped my heart out..."
Visual: Cut to a rain-slicked city street at night. The protagonist stands before a glass window pane. They are blindfolded with a translucent red silk ribbon ("Love shot me blind"). On the heavy beat accent of "You ripped my heart out", the glass window pane in front of them suddenly fractures in slow motion. Shards of glass float upward into the sky, defying gravity.
Camera Movement: Extreme close-up of the red silk blindfold, tracking backward rapidly in a whip-zoom as the glass shatters.
Lighting: High-contrast street lighting. Flashing blue and pink neon signs throw reflections onto the floating glass shards.

0:27 – 0:40 | Scene 3: The Suspended State (Pre-Chorus)
Lyrics: "This life is fake, it's so unreal / How could you leave, your time stands still"
Visual: The protagonist walks down the middle of a surreal, frozen city street. Passerby are completely frozen mid-stride like mannequins. Raindrops are suspended in mid-air, glowing like tiny diamonds.
Camera Movement: A smooth Steadicam orbit shot circling the protagonist, emphasizing their movement while the rest of the world is locked in place.
Lighting: Cold volumetric white light shafts piercing through the frozen rain, casting long, dramatic shadows.

0:40 – 0:54 | Scene 4: The Grip (Build-up)
Lyrics: "You keep me around, just to hold me down / I can't even breathe, I'm still at your mercy..."
Visual: Pale, ethereal hands made of dark, swirling ink-like smoke reach out from the shadows of the alleyways. They gently wrap around the protagonist's wrists and neck. The protagonist tilts their head back and begins to sink backward into the pavement, which has suddenly liquified into a dark pool.
Camera Movement: Low-angle shot looking up at the protagonist. The camera slowly tilts backward, mimicking the falling motion of the protagonist.
Lighting: Under-lit with a sickly, glowing cyan light, throwing sharp, stylized shadows upward across the protagonist's face.

0:54 – 1:21 | Scene 5: The Freefall (Chorus - Beat Drop)
Lyrics: "You hold me down, I'd rather love than be alone... I'll keep diving til I'm gone..."
Visual: The visual drop. The protagonist is now plunging rapidly through a deep-sea abyss. They pass glowing, bioluminescent jellyfish and submerged, glowing neon streetlights. Their clothes billow violently in the current.
Camera Movement: Fast, dynamic tracking shot looking directly down at the protagonist as they fall toward the lens. The camera spins rapidly to capture the disorientation of "diving."
Lighting: Intense, rhythmic strobing of violet and deep ocean blue. Sharp crimson light rays burst periodically from the protagonist's chest, mimicking a frantic heartbeat.

1:21 – 1:35 | Scene 6: The Drift (Verse 2)
Lyrics: "Lost with the time, slipped away... I'll rest assured, wait for days..."
Visual: The chaos slows down. The protagonist gently lands on the ocean floor, which transitions into a carpeted bedroom floor. Dust motes float in the water like tiny golden stars. They lie still, staring up at the surface.
Camera Movement: A slow, lateral dolly shot gliding left-to-right across the protagonist's profile.
Lighting: Soft, diffused moonlight filtering down from the "water surface" above, creating moving caustic patterns across the bedroom/seabed.

1:35 – 1:49 | Scene 7: The Insomnia (Verse 2 Part 2)
Lyrics: "It's just so boring losing sleep, so sick of life with no heartbeat..."
Visual: The scene shifts fully back to a real bedroom. The protagonist is lying awake, staring blankly at the ceiling. An X-ray style VFX overlay glides over their chest, showing a hollow, dark space where their heart should be—completely empty and silent.
Camera Movement: A slow vertical pan up from the empty chest cavity to their expressionless, tired eyes.
Lighting: Cold, desaturated blue light casting from an off-camera television screen, flickering rhythmically.

1:49 – 2:02 | Scene 8: The Re-Submersion (Pre-Chorus 2)
Lyrics: "You keep me around, just to hold me down..."
Visual: The walls of the bedroom begin to leak water. The bedsheet itself turns into a liquid wave. The smoky, dark hands reappear from beneath the mattress, pulling the protagonist down through the bed back into the dark void.
Camera Movement: High-angle wide shot showing the bed dissolving into a dark, swirling whirlpool.
Lighting: Harsh red backlight silhouette, framing the protagonist as they are dragged down.

2:02 – 2:30 | Scene 9: The Abyss (Chorus 2 - Climax)
Lyrics: "You hold me down, I'd rather love than be alone... rather love than die alone..."
Visual: The absolute peak of visual energy. The protagonist is surrounded by floating, fractured, glowing holographic memories of their partner's face. The protagonist reaches out to touch one, but on the snare hit, the memory shatters into thousands of glowing water droplets.
Camera Movement: Rapid whip-pans and fast-cutting close-ups of the shattering memories, intercut with wide shots of the protagonist spinning in the current.
Lighting: Heavy contrast, saturated crimson and electric indigo strobes that pulse exactly in sync with the song's bass drum hits.

2:30 – 2:57 | Scene 10: The Calm (Outro)
Lyrics: "You hold me down... rather love than die alone..."
Visual: The violent water currents fade into absolute stillness. The protagonist stops fighting and floats peacefully, closing their eyes. The chaotic neon lights fade out. The tiny crimson ember in their chest returns, pulsing slowly and warmly, fading out on the final note.
Camera Movement: Slow, continuous pull-back zoom, making the protagonist look smaller and smaller in the dark blue expanse.
Lighting: Minimal deep-sea blue. The warm, slow-pulsing ember in the chest acts as the final light source before fading to a total black screen.

**Production Notes for the Director**

VFX Style: To capture the gravity-defying water elements, the underwater scenes should be shot "dry-for-wet" (filming in a dark room with actors suspended on harnesses, shot in high frame rates like 120fps, using wind machines and digital water caustics projected onto them).
Color Grading: Keep the shadows deeply crushed and rich in blues/purples, while pushing the saturation of the crimson/amber lights to make the romantic/pain elements pop out aggressively.
