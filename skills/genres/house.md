# House

Four-on-the-floor euphoria—dancefloor community, warm night, and hypnotic loop visuals.

## Visual Palette
- Warm club tungsten + selective RGB; dawn sidewalk cool; sweaty skin highlights.
- Soft haze and disco practicals; avoid harsh stadium strobes unless peak drop.

## Core Motifs
- Bodies in sync, circle and line dances, DJ booth glimpses, afterglow streets.
- Looping visual refrains matching the groove.

## Camera & Pacing
- Floating steadicam through crowd; rhythmic inserts on kicks; crane lifts on peaks.
- Hold groove longer than festival EDM—hypnosis over shock cuts.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Communal release and night-long arc. Culture-respectful club worlds; no fake brand merch.
