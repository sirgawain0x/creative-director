# Folk

Quiet stakes and natural light—hands, instruments, and landscape as emotional memory.

## Visual Palette
- Warm daylight, wood, linen, soft window light; muted earth tones.
- Overcast exteriors welcome; avoid neon club looks unless contrast cutaway.

## Core Motifs
- Hands on strings, intimate rooms, porch/kitchen, landscape breathers, walking paths.
- Recurring small objects as memory anchors.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked-off intimacy, gentle dollies, natural light priority; slow zooms.
- Hold longer than pop; cut on breath and lyric feeling.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Quiet stakes, memory, place-as-character. No fake brand merch; keep faces generative-friendly.
