# Country

Open sky, lived-in interiors, and honest performance—road, porch, and land as emotional stakes.

## Visual Palette
- Warm daylight, denim/wood/dust textures; golden hour fields and neon roadside night.
- Soft window light indoors; avoid glossy pop studio unless intentional contrast.

## Core Motifs
- Truck/road, porch, dive stage, small-town main street, hands on guitar/hat.
- Landscape breathers between intimate verse close-ups.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked-off intimacy, gentle dollies, wide horizon establishes; handheld on live grit.
- Chorus: wider land or crowd; verse: faces and hands.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Memory, place, and quiet stakes. No costume cliché overload; no copyrighted lyric quotes.
