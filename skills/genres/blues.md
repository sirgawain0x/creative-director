# Blues

Road-worn honesty—heat, dust, and confession under hard or golden light.

## Visual Palette
- Amber daylight, neon bar night, wood and chrome grit; crushed shadows.
- Sweat highlights; avoid clean pop gloss.

## Core Motifs
- Crossroads/road, juke joint stage, train tracks, lone walker, guitar-as-confessor.
- Hands and face carry the story.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked-off intimacy, slow push on verses, wider land on bridges.
- Cut sparse—let phrases hang.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Confession and endurance. Place-as-character; no costume cliché overload or copyrighted lyric quotes.
