# Techno

Industrial pulse and hypnotic geometry—warehouse void, machine light, minimal human myth.

## Visual Palette
- Cold concrete, sodium/LED cyan, smoke shafts; desaturated with one accent color.
- Hard edges and deep blacks; avoid warm pop gloss.

## Core Motifs
- Warehouse expanse, machine rhythm, lone figure in void, endless corridor loops.
- Build as pressure; drop as geometric release.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked wide geometry, slow dollies, rhythmic strobe-synced cuts on kicks.
- Minimal whip-zoom gimmicks—hypnosis over chaos.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Alienation or transcendence through repetition. Faceless-enough leads; practical industrial spaces over cartoon CGI.
