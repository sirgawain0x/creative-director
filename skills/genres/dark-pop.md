# Dark-pop / electronic

Aligned with moody electronic dark-pop: emotional gravity, water, neon, hollow interior space.

## Palette
- Deep navy and black, wet streets, analog grain, a single warm accent (crimson ember, sickly cyan, violet strobe).
- Practical neon and volumetric shafts; avoid sunny daylight unless it is a memory shatter.

## Motifs
- Suspension, drowning, frozen time, hollow chest / missing heartbeat, ink-smoke hands, bioluminescence.
- Chorus/drop: freefall or plunge; outro: stillness and a fading pulse of light.

## Camera
- Crane descents, Steadicam orbits, whip-zooms on hits, slow pull-backs in outros.
- Lighting named per scene (under-lit cyan, caustic moonlight, strobing violet).

## Writer
Narrative of dependency and descent, not a plot dump. Keep the protagonist singular and faceless-enough for generative video.
