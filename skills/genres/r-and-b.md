# R&B

Intimate heat and velvet shadow—mood-first storytelling with soft bodies and late-night light.

## Visual Palette
- Warm tungsten, amber skin light, velvet shadow; brass or wine accents.
- Low practicals, soft haze; avoid harsh daylight unless a memory cutaway.

## Core Motifs
- Close duets, lone silhouette in doorway, city window glow, slow dance intimacy.
- Hands, fabric, breath—tactile detail over spectacle.

## Camera & Pacing
- Slow orbits, gentle push-ins, soft rack focus; hold on faces longer than pop.
- Verse: tighter. Chorus: slightly wider room geography, still intimate.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Desire, tension, or reconciliation in images. Restraint over flash; faceless-enough leads when needed for generative video.
