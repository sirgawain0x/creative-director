# K-Pop

Precision choreography, concept color, and set geometry—hook visuals that read as formation.

## Visual Palette
- Glossy high-key or concept-coded night; one or two signature palette keys per section.
- Clean reflections, controlled practicals; avoid muddy documentary grit unless concept.

## Core Motifs
- Formation refrains, costume/prop beats on hooks, mirror/studio/set transitions.
- Verse: closer character beats. Chorus: full formation geography.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked marks, whip pans on hits, steadicam orbit of formations; crisp downbeat cuts.
- Keep faces clear and bodies generative-video-friendly (readable silhouettes).

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Concept clarity over plot dump. Emotional want still present; no copyrighted lyric quotes or fake brand merch.
