# Amapiano

Log-drum pulse made visual: cool interiors, sunset exteriors, unhurried groove and social dance.

## Visual Palette
- Soft dusk gold, cool interior blues, clean nightclub tungsten; linen and chrome accents.
- Smooth gradients; avoid harsh strobe unless a drop beat.

## Core Motifs
- Lounge-to-dancefloor glide, circle dances, patio/villa frames, car cruising.
- Hands in the air on log-drum hits; shared groove over solo flex.

## Camera & Pacing
- Floating steadicam, gentle crane; rhythmic inserts on piano/log accents.
- Hold longer than EDM—groove is the pacing authority.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Social ease and late-day glow. Keep bodies readable; no fake brand merch or tourist stereotypes.
