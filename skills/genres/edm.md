# EDM

Drop architecture made visual—bodies, light, and scale that explode on the hit.

## Visual Palette
- Club RGB, lasers, wet floors, dawn-after exteriors; high saturation accents.
- Volumetric haze; avoid flat white LED wash.

## Core Motifs
- Build suspension → drop freefall; crowd as organism; DJ booth as distant altar.
- Euphoria vs comedown exteriors for outro.

## Camera & Pacing
- Sweeping cranes, rhythmic jump cuts on kicks, whip-zooms on drops.
- Build: slow push. Drop: hard cuts and scale jumps.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Physical joy and release; faceless-enough for generative video. Spectacle serving emotion, not empty FX.
