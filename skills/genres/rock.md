# Rock

Stage heat, road grit, and chorus release—conflict and catharsis in practical spaces.

## Visual Palette
- Stage tungsten, smoke, daylight grit; denim/leather textures and amp glow.
- High contrast live light; avoid clean corporate LED walls unless ironic.

## Core Motifs
- Rehearsal room, van/road, pit energy, crowd surge on chorus, guitar-as-weapon intimacy.
- Verse conflict → chorus release in geography and scale.

## Camera & Pacing
- Handheld pit energy, slow push on bridges, crash cuts on hits.
- Wider chorus; tighter verse faces and frets.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Conflict and release; avoid copyrighted lyric quotes and fake brand merch. Keep bodies readable in smoke.
