# Afrobeats

Sun-soaked motion, community dance, and city-to-coast color—celebration with local specificity.

## Visual Palette
- Saturated local color, golden hour, market/street practicals, bright fabrics.
- High energy contrast; avoid generic “party stock” cyan washes.

## Core Motifs
- Dance circles, procession, rooftop/courtyard gatherings, city-to-landscape cuts.
- Footwork and body rhythm as the visual hook.

## Camera & Pacing
- Wide establishing + close rhythm cuts on percussion; steadicam weave through crowds.
- Chorus: open geography and sync’d bodies; verse: closer faces and detail.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Culture-specific motifs without costume cliché; respect locality. Joy and community over tourist gaze.
