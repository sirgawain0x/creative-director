# Latin

Warmth, rhythm, and place—street color and romantic gravity without postcard cliché.

## Visual Palette
- Golden hour exteriors, saturated walls, night neon with warm practicals.
- Rich reds/ochres vs cool moonlight; keep one wardrobe color repeating.

## Core Motifs
- Pair dance, street procession, balcony/rooftop longing, family/celebration tableaus.
- Rhythm as geography: feet, hands, instruments in frame.

## Camera & Pacing
- Wide place-establishes + intimate twos; cuts on percussion and brass hits.
- Steadicam weave through celebration; slow push on romantic bridges.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Place-as-character and emotional want. Culture-specific motifs without costume cliché; no copyrighted lyric quotes.
