# Soul

Lived-in warmth: church-adjacent glow, street honesty, and performance that feels earned.

## Visual Palette
- Golden tungsten, wood grain, sun-through-blinds; rich earth and brass tones.
- Soft bounce light; avoid sterile studio white.

## Core Motifs
- Call-and-response energy, porch/stoop, band pocket, communal chorus swell.
- Hands on instruments, sweat-and-light performance honesty.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked-off intimacy mixed with slow dollies; wider on gospel-adjacent lifts.
- Cut on groove, not every snare—let phrases breathe.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Testimony and release over plot twists. Respect spiritual motifs without costume cliché.
