# Jazz

Smoke, brass, and time stretch—club intimacy and improvisation made spatial.

## Visual Palette
- Warm tungsten, brass highlights, velvet shadow, soft haze shafts.
- Night club booths and rain-slick streets; avoid harsh daylight unless memory.

## Core Motifs
- Solo spotlight, duo interplay, late-night streets, instrument close-ups as conversation.
- Silence and space as visual rests.

## Camera & Pacing
- Slow orbits, soft rack focus, locked-off performance holds; gentle dollies.
- Cut with phrasing, not metronome—let solos breathe.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Mood-first narrative; restraint over spectacle. Intimate stakes; generative-friendly faces in low light.
