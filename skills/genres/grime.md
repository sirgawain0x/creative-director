# Grime

UK night urgency—estate sodium, MCing heat, and restless camera on the 140 pulse.

## Visual Palette
- Sodium orange vs cold LED; wet pavement, bus stops, tower-block night.
- High contrast practicals; avoid glossy US club RGB unless deliberate clash.

## Core Motifs
- Spit-to-camera, corner cypher, night bus, stairwell bounce, crew surround.
- Energy spike on bars; city as antagonist and stage.

## Camera & Pacing
- Handheld restlessness, low-angle wides, crash zooms on punchlines.
- Faster cut density than drill; keep faces readable.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Voice and place first. No fake brand merch; respect locality without tourist cliché.
