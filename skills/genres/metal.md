# Metal

Crush, ritual, and void—high-contrast power without cartoon CGI excess.

## Visual Palette
- High-contrast B/W or desaturated cold steel; strobe and ember accents.
- Industrial spaces, smoke, hard shadows; avoid sunny pastoral unless irony.

## Core Motifs
- Ritual circle, void plunge, industrial crush, crowd wall of bodies on blasts.
- Instrument close-ups as weapons; face grit on screams/bridges.

## Camera & Pacing
- Aggressive push-ins, whip pans on blasts, locked wide on pit chaos.
- Sync hits hard; let breakdowns breathe with slower camera.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Power and catharsis; keep practical, not cartoon CGI. No copyrighted lyric quotes.
