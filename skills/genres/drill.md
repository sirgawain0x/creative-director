# Drill

Cold streets, sparse menace, and bar-synced presence—low-angle power without cartoon violence.

## Visual Palette
- Night exteriors, cold sodium/LED, desaturated estates and concrete; ice-blue vs amber.
- Motivated practicals; avoid sunny tourist stock.

## Core Motifs
- Block geography, car interiors, rooftop/stairwell, crew freeze on punchlines.
- Low-angle hero; sparse motion matching sparse production.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked wide threat, slow push-ins, crash zoom on bars; handheld walks.
- Cut on hi-hat/slide accents; hold stillness for tension.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Territory, loyalty, or confession in images. No graphic glamorization; no fake brand merch.
