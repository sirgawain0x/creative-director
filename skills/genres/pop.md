# Pop

Hook-forward visuals: clean geography, one wardrobe color, emotional want that reads in a still frame.

## Visual Palette
- High-key daylight or glossy night; one signature wardrobe color for continuity.
- Soft practicals and clean reflections; avoid muddy midtones.

## Core Motifs
- Visual refrain on the hook (gesture, location return, prop beat).
- Mirror / phone / stage used sparingly—one motif, not a collage.

## Camera & Pacing
- Steadicam verses; wider chorus geography; crisp cuts on downbeats.
- Keep faces and bodies generative-video-friendly (clear silhouette, limited crowd mush).

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Clear emotional want over plot dump. No copyrighted lyric quotes; no fake brand merch.
