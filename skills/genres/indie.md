# Indie

Found light, imperfect rooms, and quiet stakes—intimacy over arena spectacle.

## Visual Palette
- Soft window light, film grain, muted pastels or slightly crushed blacks.
- Practical lamps and overcast exteriors; avoid glossy commercial polish.

## Core Motifs
- Bedrooms, diners, empty venues, walking conversations, small gestures that recur.
- Landscape or city breathers as emotional punctuation.

## Camera & Pacing
- Locked-off or gentle handheld; slow zooms; hold longer than pop.
- Cuts on feeling more than every downbeat.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Quiet want and memory; place-as-character. Generative-friendly faces; no fake brand merch.
