# Gospel

Lift and light—communal voices, sacred-adjacent architecture, and release on the swell.

## Visual Palette
- Warm gold shafts, stained-glass color accents, soft white fill; wood and fabric textures.
- Brighten on chorus lifts; avoid cold cyber neon.

## Core Motifs
- Choir geography, raised hands, aisle processions, solo testimony to community embrace.
- Light as emotional character on the swell.

## Camera & Pacing
- Wide congregation establishes + intimate face testimonies; crane/lift on peaks.
- Cut with call-and-response energy; hold on release.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Testimony and communal release. Respect spiritual motifs without costume cliché or mockery.
