# Reggaeton

Night heat, perreo energy, and confident presence—club-to-street with dembow punch.

## Visual Palette
- Night club RGB + sodium street; wet asphalt, chrome, bold wardrobe color.
- High contrast; neon practicals over flat LED panels.

## Core Motifs
- Close dance, car interior flex, rooftop/block presence, crew vs solo swagger.
- Body rhythm locked to dembow; freeze or punch-in on bar accents.

## Camera & Pacing
- Low-angle hero, handheld follow, crash zoom on drops; wider chorus geography.
- Motivated practicals; avoid generic white cyc.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Boast, desire, or night-out mythology in images. No fake brand merch; keep generative-friendly silhouettes.
