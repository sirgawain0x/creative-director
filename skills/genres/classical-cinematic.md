# Classical Cinematic

Orchestral scale and painterly light—tableau, architecture, and slow emotional gravity.

## Visual Palette
- Painterly contrast: Rembrandt practicals, cool dawn, deep architectural shadow.
- Desaturated elegance with one accent (gold leaf, crimson drape); filmic grain welcome.

## Core Motifs
- Lone figure in vast space, ritual procession, nature vs monument, frozen tableaux that thaw.
- Instrument/orchestra as distant ritual, not pop stage flash.

## Camera & Pacing
- Slow cranes, locked tableaux, gentle orbits; minimal handheld shake.
- Phrase-length takes; cut on cadences and swells, not every bar.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Emotional gravity over plot dump. Monumental yet human; keep generative-friendly silhouettes in vast frames.
