# Punk

DIY urgency, sweat, and confrontation—small rooms, big attitude, cut on the snare.

## Visual Palette
- Harsh practicals, flyers-on-wall grit, blown highlights; primary color accents.
- Club toilets, basements, alley night; avoid polished corporate stages.

## Core Motifs
- Mosh intimacy, xerox aesthetic props, sprint-through-city, confrontational stare-to-lens.
- Chorus: chaos geography; verse: spit-distance faces.

## Camera & Pacing
- Shaky handheld, crash zooms, jump cuts; minimal steadicam polish.
- Cut aggressive and short—energy over beauty.

## Narrative & Stylistic Directives
Defiance and community. No fake brand merch; keep it raw and generative-readable.
