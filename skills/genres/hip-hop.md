# Hip-hop

Performance energy, location contrast, and a crew-or-solo presence that can cut on the snare.

## Palette
- Night exteriors, sodium streetlight, brick, chain-link, car interiors, club tungsten vs cold moonlight.
- Color: amber vs ice-blue; keep one wardrobe color repeating (e.g. red jacket) for continuity.

## Motifs
- Walk-and-talk / strut, cypher circle, rooftop, subway platform, low-angle hero, crowd freeze on a punchline.
- Chorus: wider geography or a practical stunt-feel move (not cartoon CGI). Verse: closer, handheld, breath.

## Camera
- Handheld tracking, low-angle wide, steadicam follow, crash zoom on bars.
- Lighting: motivated practicals; avoid generic studio cyc.

## Writer
Boast, confession, or come-up in images. No copyrighted lyric quotes; no fake brand merch.
