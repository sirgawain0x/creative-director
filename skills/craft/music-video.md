# Music video (generic)

Use this pack when the brief is not dark-pop/electronic or hip-hop.

## Treatment
- Logline, narrative arc (setup / escalation / release), and a short list of visual motifs.
- Map lyric *themes* to images. Do not quote copyrighted lyrics verbatim.
- State what not to show (logos, readable signage, celebrity likenesses).

## Picture
- Prefer a single protagonist and a repeating visual motif so Veo clips can match.
- Cuts land on beats; chorus or drop gets the highest-energy image.
- Camera and lighting must be specific enough to paste into a Veo prompt.

## Output
Return a treatment only (writer) or a beat-synced shot list (DP). Do not invent download URLs.
